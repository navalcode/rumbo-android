# Rumbo interface

Use warm paper `#FAF8F3`, charcoal `#202421` and one copper accent `#9B432F`. Outfit handles reading and controls; Fraunces supports the travel identity and destination headings. Preserve the original Canvas landscapes and the Explore / My plan / Lab destinations.

Keep the introduction compact: a short title, a permanently labelled search field, filters and results. A destination uses one image/text composition with a 2.1:1 landscape, 16 dp radius and readable region, duration and example price. Saving a favourite remains independent of opening the detail. Controls use 12 dp corners, small labels 8 dp, and touch targets at least 48 dp.

Loading, cached content, empty results, errors and local booking confirmation retain visible text and useful actions. Failed refreshes preserve the catalogue. Do not imply a live travel service: the Retrofit contract is intercepted locally and all bookings are fictional. Keep navigation IDs, Room contracts and cancellation behavior intact.

Check English/Spanish, enlarged text, search/filter, favourite, booking, Back, activity recreation and offline recovery. Only actual executed-app captures belong in `docs/images`. Generated design references are concepts, never native validation. Font licenses are bundled in APK assets and documented in THIRD_PARTY.md.

The copy review uses direct labels for the catalogue, plan and demo booking. Scenario errors identify the simulated failure and explain how to return to a successful load. Generic trip highlights and redundant loading text have been removed. Missing-trip details use a separate message from an empty filtered search.
